HexBound uses the separately released CC BY 4.0 SRD 5.1 text, without incorporating the newer 2024 ruleset. The upstream repository's historical OGL references are not presented as the license used here for the SRD text.

HexBound adds Chinese display labels, local indexes, explicit source-backed correction overlays, character growth and resource logic, bounded automation handlers, and automation metadata. These changes do not alter the licenses applicable to the underlying material. Raw JSON files remain byte-for-byte copies of the pinned upstream snapshot. Corrections and their official source pages are recorded separately in `backend/data/errata-overlay.json`.

The local catalog contains the 2014/SRD 5.1 core classes, levels, skills, spells and other reference entries. Catalog inclusion is not a claim of complete gameplay automation. Unimplemented or partly implemented effects are marked as reference or assisted content; project-specific abstract encounters, rest timing controls, scene rewards and other stated house rules are not official SRD additions.
